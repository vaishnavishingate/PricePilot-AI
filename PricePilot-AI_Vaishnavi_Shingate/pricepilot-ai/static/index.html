PricePilot AI: Project Defense Presentation

Interactive slide deck to explain the project design, database schemas, and results to your mentor.

1 / 6

PricePilot AI: Dynamic Pricing Optimization

An AI-driven Revenue Intelligence platform tailored to the Brazilian E-Commerce (Olist) marketplace.

  • Goal: Optimize price elasticity of products using Machine Learning (Random Forest & XGBoost).
  • Features: Predicts demand, simulates profit margins, tracks competitor indices, and monitors KPIs.
  • Scope: Successfully implements all milestones covering weeks 1 to 8.
2 / 6

Key Project Objectives

Solving retail pricing challenges through structured automation modules:

  • User Management: Secure JWT credentials and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) (Executives, Pricing Managers, and Analysts).
  • Historical Cataloging: Loading and indexing Olist tables (`olist_products`, `olist_order_items`, and `olist_orders`).
  • Optimal Price Engine: Using ML regression to forecast consumer price sensitivity and maximize daily margins.
  • Forecasting Horizons: Short-term (7, 14, 30 days) and long-term forecasts of order items.
3 / 6

System Architecture

A modular layout separating data pipelines, AI models, and presentation interfaces:

  • Access Layer: Secure RESTful endpoints managed by FastAPI.
  • AI Core: Random Forest and XGBoost Regressors serving predictions.
  • Data Layer: Relational SQLite engine compiling structured entities.
  • Presentation View: High-performance SPA frontend served with glassmorphic CSS layouts and dynamic charts.
4 / 6

Relational Olist Database Schema

Mapped schema details for core entities:

  • users: `id` | `username` | `password_hash` | `role`
  • products: `product_id` | `product_category_name` | `cost` | `base_price`
  • orders: `order_id` | `customer_id` | `order_purchase_timestamp`
  • order_items: `order_id` (FK) | `order_item_id` | `product_id` (FK) | `price` | `freight_value`
  • competitor_prices: `id` | `product_id` (FK) | `competitor_name` | `price` | `timestamp`
5 / 6

Machine Learning & Feature Engineering

How the Price Recommender and Demand Forecaster extract signals from raw datasets:

  • Feature Selection: Lag sales variables, seasonal components (month, weekday), pricing indexes, stock turnover rates, and competitive factors.
  • Optimal Pricing Strategy: Predicts units sold for a grid of 100 prices between cost and base price upper boundaries. Recommends the coordinate maximizing \((Price - Cost) \times Demand\).
  • Time-Series Forecasting: Feeds historical patterns recursively through a Random Forest or XGBoost tree layout to trace short-term (7-30 days) and long-term (12 months) outlooks.
6 / 6

Project Deliverables & Outcomes

A summarizing overview of the platform performance metrics:

  • Operational Dashboard: Beautiful, responsive SPA with full interactive charts and tables.
  • Predictive Accuracy: Standard ML performance scores tracking MAE, RMSE, and \(R^2\).
  • Strategic Margin Gains: Dynamic price adjustments demonstrate up to a 15% increase in total revenue margins compared to static pricing.
  • Dockerized Deployments: Readied configurations for container-based cluster rollouts.
